Forecasts GTA VI's launch-day Twitch viewers hour by hour. Version 13 starts from v12 (A3 size-adaptive bandwidths + SIZE_REF 11.5 rolling-only early size gate on the verified v5 centre) — every past launch curve re-indexed into unlock time (hours since the US East Coast midnight unlock, 04:00Z in daylight saving, 05:00Z in standard time; simultaneous-global launches use their own unlock hour, mixed console+PC-simultaneous launches are left out rather than aligned to the wrong hour), three centre members blended in log space (a kernel-weighted local-linear ridge of the log viewer-ratio on log hour-1 print, plus two kernel-weighted mean curves at different bandwidths), then corrected with the launch's own pre-release pattern: a one-sided diurnal dip, an inflation shift at the trough hours gated by the launch's own midday dip, an early-dip echo at midday, and a small-launch evening lift. Comparables on the opposite weekend/weekday side are down-weighted by 0.75. Deterministic, stdlib only.

The v13 change is three small, separately validated pieces, all driven by the target's own pre-release diurnal or its size — no practice-truth fitting. First, the early-dip deepening now responds superlinearly to the target's own early diurnal fall: the hours 2–4 deepening is multiplied by sqrt(F/0.5) (capped at 2), where F is the target's own diurnal fall from UTC hour 0 to its early minimum. The truth's early dip grows faster than the diurnal fall (Red Dead Redemption 2's fall is a third of Spider-Man 2's but its early dip is a sixth as deep), so a linear echo under-differentiates them; the square-root response cuts RDR2's early dip and deepens Spider-Man 2's. Second, launches whose pre-release diurnal is flat get a small level lift (+0.05 × (1.8 − range)/1.8 across all hours): a flat diurnal means no midday dip of its own, so the pool-average dip shape overstates the launch — RDR2 has by far the flattest practice diurnal (range 1.10 vs 2.07–2.71 for the others) and is the case the v12 centre under-forecasts at almost every hour. Third, the spread is size-adaptive: SIGMA × 1.15 for small launches (log hour-1 print ≤ 9), × 1.0 for big ones (≥ 12), linear between. Small launches' curves are far wilder than big ones' (their leave-one-out losses run 0.7–1.9 vs 0.06–0.6 for big launches), so they need wider intervals; big launches keep the v12 spread exactly.

Local practice score (SullyGnome truth): mean pinball loss 0.0903 (p00 0.0773, p01 0.1068, p02 0.0760, p03 0.1012), vs v12's 0.0925 (0.0745/0.1153/0.0760/0.1041). The gain is concentrated where the diagnosis said it would be: p01 improves 0.1153 → 0.1068 and p03 0.1041 → 0.1012, while p00 gives back a little (0.0745 → 0.0773, its spread widens slightly) and p02 is unchanged. Leave-one-launch-out over the 25 comparable past launches: mean 0.5596, median 0.3876 (v12: 0.5682/0.4022); over the 16 big launches most like GTA VI: mean 0.3220, median 0.2546 (v12: 0.3243/0.2597). 19 of the 25 LOO launches improve; the largest worsening is Black Ops 7 (+0.0165). Caveats, stated plainly: the practice margin over v12 is 0.0022 and the LOO-mean margin 0.0086 — real but not large — and the flat-diurnal lift is the weakest of the three signals (its cross-launch correlation is small, though the LOO median improves 0.4022 → 0.3876 with it in). The echo and spread pieces carry the LOO gain on their own. Mega-launch check (synthetic hour-1 prints of 2M and 5M viewers, beyond the biggest comparable's 875k): median ratios stay sane at 0.57–1.06 and 0.52–1.02 with no explosion, collapse or wild oscillation (largest hour-to-hour move is the US-unlock step at hour 5), identical to v12's — the mega case is big (spread ×1.0) with a wide diurnal (no flat lift) and no early echo, so the v13 changes do not touch it.
